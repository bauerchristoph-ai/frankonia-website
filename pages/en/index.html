Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a security service cost per hour?

A qualified security service in Bavaria typically costs between €22 and €42 per hour, depending on qualification, deployment location, and requirements. Standard object protection with certified personnel usually starts around €28–30 per hour.

What's the difference between Industrial Site Security and Property Protection?

Industrial Site Security refers to on-site protection for industrial and commercial premises, typically with a fixed team. Property Protection is broader property protection, covering buildings and facilities of any type, including patrols and access control.

How quickly can FRANKONIA provide security staff?

For standard assignments, FRANKONIA can typically deploy certified security personnel within 48–72 hours. Urgent requests, including Fire Watch and Intervention Service, can often be arranged faster — ask your contact person for current availability.

Are FRANKONIA's security staff certified?

Yes. FRANKONIA is certified to DIN 77200-1 and DIN EN ISO 9001, and all personnel undergo background checks and role-specific training before deployment.

Which regions does FRANKONIA serve?

FRANKONIA operates within roughly 100 km of Bamberg, covering Franconia and Bavaria — including Nuremberg, Würzburg, and Erlangen. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location.

What happens during the free security analysis?

A FRANKONIA security expert reviews your current setup — coverage gaps, compliance status, and risk areas — in a focused 20-minute conversation. You'll receive a written recommendation with no obligation to continue.

Does FRANKONIA offer short-term or one-off assignments?

Yes. Services such as Fire Watch and Event Security are commonly booked for defined, short-term periods, alongside long-term contracts for ongoing Industrial Site Security and Property Protection.

Is there a single point of contact for my account?

Every FRANKONIA client is assigned one dedicated contact person responsible for coordination, reporting, and any changes to your security setup — no call centers or rotating staff.

How are security personnel selected and trained?

All candidates undergo background screening, certification checks under §34a GewO, and role-specific onboarding before their first shift, with ongoing training to maintain DIN 77200-1 compliance.

What contract terms does FRANKONIA offer?

FRANKONIA offers both flexible short-term agreements and longer-term contracts, depending on the service. Terms are discussed individually during the free security analysis.
