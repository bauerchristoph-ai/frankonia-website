What is Werkschutz?

Werkschutz is on-site industrial security: a dedicated, certified team protecting a company's premises, personnel, and assets around the clock. It combines access control, patrols, and incident response tailored to industrial and manufacturing environments.

Werkschutz differs from general property protection mainly in scope: it's built around a single industrial or commercial site, typically staffed by a fixed, site-trained team rather than rotating patrol coverage. That consistency matters for sites with specific access rules, hazardous areas, or shift patterns that a rotating guard wouldn't already know.

FRANKONIA provides Werkschutz for manufacturing plants, logistics centers, and industrial campuses across Franconia and Bavaria, with personnel certified under DIN 77200-1.

When Do Companies Need Werkschutz?

Werkschutz becomes a priority once a site's size, hours, or risk profile outgrow informal or part-time coverage. Common situations include:

  • Industrial premises with valuable equipment or materials

    Machinery, raw materials, or inventory that would be costly to replace or dangerous if tampered with.

  • Production sites running outside normal business hours

    Night shifts, weekend operations, or fully unattended periods where the site still needs coverage.

  • Access control for employees, contractors, and visitors

    A single site with multiple types of legitimate visitors needs a consistent, enforced entry process.

  • Recurring unauthorized entry or trespassing incidents

    Sites that have already experienced break-ins, trespassing, or perimeter breaches.

  • Incident prevention for high-risk or regulated processes

    Operations where a security or safety incident carries outsized financial or regulatory consequences.

  • Documentation requirements for insurance or compliance audits

    Insurers or auditors requiring proof of consistent, documented site security.

What Werkschutz Includes

Exact scope is defined per site during the security analysis. A typical FRANKONIA Werkschutz deployment includes:

Access & Visitor Control

Checking credentials and logging entry for employees, contractors, and visitors at the site's access points.

Patrols

Scheduled and randomized patrols across the premises, including perimeter and high-risk areas.

Gatehouse Duties

Staffing entry points, managing barriers, and coordinating deliveries or shift-change traffic.

Incident Reporting

Documenting irregularities, safety hazards, or security incidents as they're observed.

Alarm Response Coordination

First response to triggered alarms on-site, coordinated with your existing alarm system.

Documentation

Digital shift logs covering patrols, incidents, and access activity, available for review at any time.
